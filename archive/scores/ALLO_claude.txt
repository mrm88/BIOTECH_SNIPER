1. BASE RATES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

(a) Historical Allogeneic CAR-T Trial Success Rates in Lymphoma
   - UCART19 (Cellectis): 48% ORR in r/r B-ALL, median PFS 2.1 months, median 
     persistence 28 days. 56% expansion rate. Multiple deaths from infections.
     [Source: Lancet Haematology, CALM trial, NCT02746952]
   - PBCAR19B: 71% ORR, 43% CR in 13 patients (Phase 1). [Frontiers, 2025]
   - CB-010 (Caribou/CRISPR): 94% ORR in ANTLER Phase 1 for B-NHL. No GvHD.
     [Frontiers in Immunology, 2025]
   - Cema-cel/ALLO-501 (Allogene): 58% ORR, 42% CR overall; 67% ORR, 58% CR at 
     selected Phase 2 dose in r/r LBCL. 100% CR in low disease burden subgroup.
     Median DOR 23.1 months in CR patients. Persistence up to 4 months.
     [JCO, Feb 2025; Allogene PR, Feb 2025]
   - Base rate for allo CAR-T "meaningful efficacy" in lymphoma: ~35-45%
     (range across programs, heavily dependent on product and lymphodepletion)

(b) MRD Clearance Improvement Rates in LBCL Consolidation Studies
   - After R-CHOP completion: ~24% of DLBCL patients remain MRD-positive (ctDNA).
     [Oncology News Central / JCO Wang et al., 2025]
   - MRD-positive patients post-R-CHOP: 2-year PFS 28% vs 88% MRD-negative.
     [JCO PhasED-Seq validation, HR 9.7, P<.0001]
   - Spontaneous MRD clearance in observation: Allogene management estimates 
     1-2 out of 12 patients (~8-17%). [MarketBeat/TD Cowen, March 2026]
   - Autologous CAR-T (ZUMA-12) in high-risk 1L LBCL: 86% CR, 92% ORR at 3-year
     follow-up — but this is a DIFFERENT population (not MRD-only). [PubMed, 2025]
   - No direct precedent for allo CAR-T MRD clearance in 1L consolidation LBCL.

(c) Phase 2 Interim Futility Pass Rates Generally
   - Futility analyses are designed by sponsors to be passable if the drug has 
     any meaningful activity. Typical pass rate: ~65-75% for drugs that reach 
     Phase 2. However, this assumes a validated mechanism with prior clinical POC.
   - For novel mechanisms / unproven platforms: futility pass rate drops to ~40-55%.
   - Allogeneic CAR-T is a partially validated platform (proven activity, unproven 
     durability in this specific setting).
   - Estimated base rate for this type of interim: ~50-60%.

(d) CD19-Targeting Rates in MRD-Positive Post-R-CHOP Patients
   - No direct data exists for CD19 CAR-T MRD clearance in post-R-CHOP MRD+ LBCL.
   - Nearest analog: autologous CD19 CAR-T in pediatric B-ALL MRD+ patients 
     achieved 96% MRD-negative CR. [Frontiers in Immunology, 2025]
   - Cema-cel 100% CR in low disease burden r/r LBCL (n=6). [JCO, 2025]
   - CD19 is almost universally expressed on LBCL cells; target availability 
     is not a concern. The question is whether allo CAR-T can expand and persist 
     long enough to clear MRD.

2. FACTOR ANALYSIS (Weighted)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Starting Base Rate: 35% (historical allo CAR-T trial success in lymphoma)

FACTOR 1: Prior Allo CAR-T Data Quality for Cema-cel (25% weight)
   Score: STRONG POSITIVE (+12 ppts)
   Rationale:
   - Cema-cel has the BEST-IN-CLASS allogeneic CAR-T dataset in LBCL:
     67% ORR, 58% CR at selected dose, median DOR 23.1 months in CR.
   - Zero high-grade CRS, zero ICANS, zero GvHD. [JCO, Feb 2025]
   - 100% CR in low disease burden patients (n=6) — directly relevant to 
     ALPHA3's MRD-only population. [Allogene PR, Feb 2025]
   - Durable remissions beyond 4 years in some patients.
   - Largest and longest allo CAR-T dataset in LBCL (87 patients enrolled).
   - This is meaningfully better data than UCART19 or other allo programs.
   Adjusted: 35% → 47%

FACTOR 2: Biological Plausibility of MRD Clearance with Allo CAR-T (20% weight)
   Score: MODERATE POSITIVE (+8 ppts)
   Rationale:
   - MRD in post-R-CHOP LBCL represents MINIMAL disease — exactly where 
     cema-cel showed 100% CR rates in Phase 1.
   - MRD clearance requires initial CAR-T expansion and cytotoxic burst, 
     NOT necessarily long-term persistence. Allo CAR-T expands adequately 
     in first 2-4 weeks.
   - CD19 is the most validated CAR-T target; mechanism is proven.
   - Patients are treatment-naive to CAR-T, with functional immune systems 
     (just completed R-CHOP), reducing T-cell exhaustion concern.
   - HOWEVER: some MRD cells may reside in nodal sanctuary sites where 
     CAR-T trafficking may be limited.
   Adjusted: 47% → 55%

FACTOR 3: Stringency of 25-30% Absolute Improvement Bar (20% weight)
   Score: SLIGHTLY NEGATIVE (-2 ppts)
   Rationale:
   - In n=12 per arm: 25% absolute difference = 3 patients.
   - If observation has 1/12 (8%) clearance, cema-cel needs ≥4/12 (33%).
   - If observation has 2/12 (17%) clearance, cema-cel needs ≥5/12 (42%).
   - Management chose this bar by benchmarking to:
     * Pola-R-CHP 6.3% PFS improvement in frontline [TD Cowen, March 2026]
     * 2L autologous CAR-T ~30% response improvement [TD Cowen, March 2026]
     * Bladder cancer MRD study 11% MRD clearance difference [TD Cowen]
   - The bar is designed to be clinically meaningful, not trivially low.
   - Small sample (n=12/arm) creates HIGH VARIANCE — could randomly miss 
     even if true effect is real. Or randomly pass if true effect is modest.
   - CRITICAL: n=12 per arm means ~8.3 percentage point granularity.
     Difference must be at least 3 patients out of 12.
   Adjusted: 55% → 53%

FACTOR 4: Persistence Challenge for Allogeneic CAR-T (20% weight)
   Score: MODERATE NEGATIVE (-8 ppts)
   Rationale:
   - THE existential risk. Allo CAR-T has a well-documented persistence 
     problem driven by host T-cell-mediated rejection of donor cells.
   - UCART19: median persistence 28 days, only 5/14 patients had cells 
     beyond day 42. [Lancet Haematology, 2022]
   - Cema-cel: persistence up to 4 months (better than UCART19) but still 
     far below autologous CAR-T (months to years). [JCO, 2025]
   - Allogene's solution: FCA90 lymphodepletion (adding ALLO-647 anti-CD52)
     to suppress host rejection. ALPHA3 uses standard FC only (no ALLO-647 
     in the interim analysis cohort), which may REDUCE CAR-T persistence.
   - IMPORTANT NUANCE: For MRD clearance, persistence may be LESS critical 
     than for durable tumor control. MRD cells are few; initial expansion 
     may suffice to clear them. This partially mitigates the persistence risk.
   - But if MRD represents resistant clones or cells in protected niches, 
     short persistence = incomplete clearance.
   Adjusted: 53% → 45%

FACTOR 5: Patient Selection Quality — MRD+ Enrichment (15% weight)
   Score: POSITIVE (+7 ppts)
   Rationale:
   - Brilliant trial design. MRD-positive enrichment:
     * Selects patients with proven chemo-sensitive disease (responded to R-CHOP)
     * Low tumor burden (CR/PR by PET, only MRD detectable by ctDNA)
     * Unequivocal unmet need (MRD+ = 2yr PFS of only 28%) [JCO, 2025]
     * Treatment-naive to CAR-T (optimal T-cell fitness in these patients)
   - FORESIGHT CLARITY MRD test (PhasED-Seq) is ultrasensitive and validated.
   - "7th cycle" positioning is elegant for adoption (no workflow disruption).
   - Community cancer center administration = broader access narrative.
   - Management can point to 1-2 spontaneous clearances in observation arm 
     as the control rate, making the bar more achievable.
   Adjusted: 45% → 52%

FACTOR-ADJUSTED PROBABILITY: 52%

3. ITERATIVE MONTE CARLO SIMULATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Methodology:
- 200,000 iterations per meta-run, 100 meta-runs varying scenario weights
- Three scenarios: Bull (15-30% weight), Base (25-55%), Bear (20-40%)
- Each simulation: draw true clearance rates from triangular distributions, 
  then simulate binomial outcomes for 12 patients per arm
- Success: absolute MRD clearance difference ≥ 25%

Scenario Assumptions:

│ Scenario │ Cema-cel Clearance Range  │ Observation Range      │
├──────────┼───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┤

│ BASE     │ 28-58% (mode 40%)         │ 4-22% (mode 12%)      │
│ BEAR     │ 8-38% (mode 20%)          │ 4-25% (mode 12%)      │
└──────────┴───────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘

Results:
- P(pass at ≥25% bar): 60.8% [80% CI: 57.1% – 65.1%]
- P(pass at ≥30% bar): 47.9% [80% CI: 43.6% – 52.3%]

Scenario Breakdown:
  BULL  scenario: 95.8% pass rate (allo CAR-T fully works in low burden)
  BASE  scenario: 71.1% pass rate (moderate effect, some persistence limitation)
  BEAR  scenario: 28.0% pass rate (persistence fails, minimal clearance advantage)

Simulated MRD Clearance Rates:
  Cema-cel arm:    42% (median 42%)
  Observation arm: 12% (median 8%)
  Mean absolute difference: 30%

Analysis (52%, weight 40%) = 57.3%, rounded to 55% reflecting additional 
conservatism for: (a) first-ever allo CAR-T randomized trial in this setting, 
(b) n=12/arm high variance, and (c) lack of ALLO-647 in interim cohort.

The 80% CI of 45%-65% reflects genuine model uncertainty — the bull case 
(allo works in low burden as Phase 1 suggests) is plausible but so is the 
bear case (persistence insufficient even for MRD clearance).

CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM
- Significant prior data exists for cema-cel, reducing uncertainty vs a 
  first-in-human program.
- But zero precedent for allo CAR-T in randomized MRD-guided consolidation.
- n=12/arm creates inherent stochastic noise that could swing results ±15%.

UPSIDE DRIVERS (if passed)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • 100% CR in low disease burden in Phase 1 is directly translatable to 
    MRD-only population — this is the single strongest bullish datapoint.
  • MRD clearance does NOT require long persistence; initial CAR-T expansion 
    burst may be sufficient, partially neutralizing the persistence concern.
  • Trial design is MRD-enriched — selecting for patients most likely to 
    benefit from any anti-CD19 therapy.
  • Zero high-grade CRS/ICANS in Phase 1 supports clean safety in 1L, 
    which matters for community oncology adoption narrative.
  • Observation arm spontaneous clearance likely very low (management 
    guided to 1-2/12), making the bar more achievable.
  • Management set the 25-30% bar themselves — they have seen early 
    unblinded data patterns from site enrollment and may have calibrated 
    the bar to what they believe is achievable (positive signal).
  • "7th cycle" framing + off-the-shelf delivery = massive commercial 
    opportunity if efficacy proven (>$2B peak sales per management).
  • If passed: stock likely +50-80% given $2.33 share price and 
    $8-14 analyst price targets.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DOWNSIDE RISKS (if failed)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Allogeneic CAR-T persistence remains the #1 risk. Cema-cel persistence 
    is only ~4 months (vs years for autologous). UCART19 showed persistence 
    of only 28 days. Host T-cell rejection may eliminate cema-cel before 
    MRD is fully cleared.
  • ALPHA3 interim uses FC-only lymphodepletion (no ALLO-647), potentially 
    reducing the depth of host immune suppression and allowing faster 
    rejection of allo CAR-T cells. Phase 1 best data used FCA90.
  • n=12 per arm creates extreme stochastic risk. One unlucky randomization 
    (e.g., 3 spontaneous clearances in observation) could kill the delta 
    even if cema-cel works. Conversely, one more cema-cel non-responder 
    could tank the rate.
  • No precedent: This is the FIRST randomized allo CAR-T trial in MRD-
    guided 1L consolidation. Zero external validation points exist.
  • Competitors with AUTOLOGOUS CAR-T (better persistence) have struggled:
    * BELINDA (tisa-cel 2L): FAILED to beat SOC
    * Autologous CAR-T consolidation studies have had mixed results
    * If autologous struggles, allogeneic faces even higher bar
  • Historical "show-me" story: ALLO has disappointed multiple times. 
    Market has low confidence. Failure = -60% to -80% stock move.
  • MRD assay variability: PhasED-Seq is ultrasensitive but false positives/
    negatives in 12 patients could meaningfully distort results.
  • Cash runway into Q1 2028 ($258M), but failure would likely require 
    significant restructuring or additional dilutive financing.
