(b) ADAPT SERON Trial Results
─────────────────────────────
• ADAPT SERON met primary endpoint: Statistically significant
  improvement in MG-ADL total score at Day 29 (p=0.0068).
  [Source: argenx Press Release, GlobeNewswire, Jan 13, 2026]
• Mean improvement: 3.35 points on MG-ADL — clinically meaningful.
• Improvements in MG-ADL and QMG scores across ALL subgroups:
  MuSK+, LRP4+, and triple seronegative.
• Improvements sustained across subsequent treatment cycles in
  open-label extension (Part B).
• Safety profile CONSISTENT with established VYVGART profile in
  AChR-Ab seropositive gMG. No new safety concerns.
  [Source: argenx sBLA Acceptance PR, Jan 13, 2026]
• n=119 patients across North America, Europe, China, Middle East.

  BASE RATE (given met primary endpoint + clean safety): ~87-92%

(c) FcRn Inhibitor Class in gMG — Regulatory Precedent
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Efgartigimod (VYVGART): Approved Dec 2021 for AChR-Ab+ gMG.
  First FcRn blocker approved. Now also approved for CIDP.
• Rozanolixizumab (Rystiggo/UCB): Approved June 2023 for gMG in
  AChR+ AND MuSK+ patients. FIRST therapy approved for MuSK+ gMG.
  [Source: FDA Label/Rystiggo, accessdata.fda.gov]
  [Source: Immunotherapy/PMC12045563]
• Key: Rozanolixizumab is already approved for MuSK+ gMG — a subset
  of the seronegative population. This establishes regulatory
  precedent that FcRn inhibitors work in non-AChR-Ab+ gMG.
• HOWEVER: Rozanolixizumab is NOT approved for LRP4+ or triple
  seronegative patients. VYVGART would be FIRST approved therapy
  for these populations — genuine unmet need.
  [Source: VA Drug Monograph/Rozanolixizumab]

  BASE RATE (FcRn inhibitor, expanding gMG subtype label): ~88-92%

FACTOR 1: Pivotal Data Strength (Weight: 30%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Score: 8.0/10

POSITIVES:
• ADAPT SERON primary endpoint met with p=0.0068 — robust statistical
  significance (well below 0.05 threshold).
• 3.35-point MG-ADL improvement is clinically meaningful (MCID for
  MG-ADL is typically 2-3 points).
• Consistent effect across ALL seronegative subtypes (MuSK+, LRP4+,
  triple seronegative) — important for labeling discussions.
• Safety profile clean, consistent with known VYVGART profile. No
  new safety signals.
  [Source: argenx Press Release, Jan 13, 2026]
• Open-label extension showed sustained and improving responses
  across subsequent treatment cycles.
• Independent open-label study (PMC12579174, n=30 SN MG patients):
  72% responder rate on MGII, 76% on MG-ADL. MG-ADL improved by
  3.4 points — highly consistent with ADAPT SERON.
  [Source: Therap Advances Neuro Disorders, PMC12579174]

NEGATIVES:
• p=0.0068 is significant but not overwhelmingly so (not p<0.001).
  In regulatory context, this is adequate but provides less buffer.
• MG-ADL improvement of 3.35 points, while clinically meaningful,
  is modest. In AChR+ ADAPT trial, MG-ADL responder rate was 68%
  in efgartigimod arm — the AChR+ population may respond better.
• Triple seronegative patients (~10% of gMG) are the most novel
  subgroup — limited precedent for ANY therapy in this population.
• Seronegative MG may be more treatment-resistant — 72% responder
  rate in open-label study, but 0% achieved symptom freedom.
  [Source: PMC12579174]
• QMG improvement was NOT significant in the open-label study (0.7
  point change, p=0.57) — though in 33% of patients, ≥3-point
  (MCID) reduction was achieved. QMG and MG-ADL can diverge.

WEIGHTED CONTRIBUTION: 8.0 × 0.30 = 2.40

RATIONALE FOR HIGH ESTIMATE (85%):
─────────────────────────────────────
This is a HIGH-CONVICTION approval call based on:

1. ESTABLISHED DRUG, ESTABLISHED SAFETY: VYVGART has been on the
   market since December 2021 in AChR+ gMG and since 2024 in CIDP.
   The safety profile is thoroughly characterized with thousands of
   patient-years of exposure. This sBLA review focuses primarily on
   efficacy in the new population.

2. POSITIVE PHASE 3 DATA: ADAPT SERON met its primary endpoint
   (p=0.0068) with clinically meaningful effect size (3.35-point
   MG-ADL improvement). Consistent effects across all seronegative
   subtypes. This is the data package FDA needs.

3. REGULATORY PRECEDENT: Rozanolixizumab was approved in June 2023
   for AChR+ AND MuSK+ gMG, establishing that FcRn inhibitors work
   in non-AChR populations. The biological rationale (IgG reduction)
   is the same regardless of autoantibody subtype.

4. PRIORITY REVIEW + NO ADCOM: FDA's own assessment indicated this
   application merits expedited review and does not require external
   expert panel input. This is among the strongest regulatory signals
   possible.

5. UNMET NEED: LRP4+ and triple seronegative patients have NO
   approved treatments. FDA has historically been receptive to
   approving drugs for underserved rare disease subpopulations.

WHY NOT HIGHER (>90%):
• p=0.0068 is significant but with less buffer than p<0.001. FDA
  reviewers may scrutinize subgroup consistency more carefully.
• Triple seronegative is truly novel — no drug has been approved for
  this population. FDA may have questions about mechanism of action
  in patients with no detectable IgG autoantibodies.
• Part A of ADAPT SERON was relatively short (4 weeks of treatment).
  FDA may want more durability data, potentially as a post-marketing
  requirement rather than blocking approval.
• Non-zero CMC/labeling risk exists for any sBLA, even clean ones.
• Historical sBLA approval rate is ~85-90%, not 100%. Surprises happen.
