SECTION 1: BASE RATES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

(a) Anti-IGF-1R Antibodies in Chronic TED — Historical Success
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Tepezza (teprotumumab): First approved anti-IGF-1R for active TED
  (2020). Showed efficacy in chronic TED in exploratory studies, but
  limited formal pivotal data in chronic population specifically.
• Veligrotug (VRDN-001, IV): THRIVE-2 Phase 3 in CHRONIC TED — met
  ALL primary and secondary endpoints. PRR 56% vs 8% placebo
  (p<0.0001). First global Phase 3 to show statistically significant
  diplopia response and resolution in chronic TED.
  [Source: Viridian THRIVE-2 Topline, Dec 2024]
• Veligrotug THRIVE (active TED): Also met all endpoints. PRR similar
  magnitude. Consistent anti-IGF-1R class effect across disease phases.
• Anti-IGF-1R mechanism is now VALIDATED in both active and chronic TED
  across multiple Phase 3 trials.

  BASE RATE (anti-IGF-1R in chronic TED, validated mechanism): ~70-80%

(b) Elegrobart Specifically — Sister Trial REVEAL-1 in Active TED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• REVEAL-1 topline data reported TODAY (March 30, 2026):
  - Primary endpoint MET: Q4W PRR 54% vs 18% placebo (p<0.0001)
  - Q8W PRR: 63% vs 18% placebo
  - Complete diplopia resolution: 51% Q4W vs 16% placebo
  [Source: Business Wire/Viridian, March 30, 2026]
• CRITICAL: REVEAL-1 confirmed elegrobart has robust efficacy in
  active TED — the SC formulation WORKS.
• HOWEVER: Market sold off ~32-41% on safety signal — hearing
  impairment (tinnitus). Q4W arm: 11.3% placebo-adjusted rate;
  Q8W arm: 2.3% placebo-adjusted rate. All were tinnitus; none
  associated with hearing reductions.
  [Source: AInvest, Tickeron, March 30, 2026]
• Key comparison with veligrotug hearing impairment rates:
  - THRIVE (active TED): 5.5% placebo-adjusted
  - THRIVE-2 (chronic TED): 9.6% placebo-adjusted (13% vs 3%)
  [Source: Viridian THRIVE-2 Topline Deck, Dec 2024]
• Elegrobart Q4W hearing impairment (11.3%) is modestly higher than
  veligrotug in chronic TED (9.6%), but Q8W (2.3%) is much lower.
• This is a CLASS EFFECT, not elegrobart-specific. Same binding domain
  as veligrotug. SC vs IV route may affect PK/tissue exposure.

  BASE RATE (elegrobart specifically, post-REVEAL-1): ~65-75%

(c) Active-to-Chronic TED Translation
──────────────────────────────────────
• Active TED: CAS ≥3, onset within 15 months. More inflammatory.
• Chronic TED: Any CAS, onset >15 months. More fibrotic, harder to
  reverse tissue remodeling. BUT anti-IGF-1R addresses the underlying
  orbital fibroblast pathology, not just inflammation.
• Veligrotug THRIVE → THRIVE-2 translation: BOTH succeeded. This is
  the strongest analog for REVEAL-1 → REVEAL-2 translation.
• Tepezza showed some chronic TED benefit, though not in formal
  registrational study.
• Historically, TED drugs that work in active phase have shown
  directionally consistent efficacy in chronic phase, though sometimes
  with attenuated effect sizes.

  BASE RATE (active → chronic translation, anti-IGF-1R): ~70-75%

COMPOSITE BASE RATE: ~70%

FACTOR 1: Prior Data Strength / Mechanism Validation (Weight: 30%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Score: 8.5/10

POSITIVES (STRONG):
• Anti-IGF-1R is now the MOST validated mechanism in TED:
  - Tepezza approved (active TED)
  - Veligrotug THRIVE + THRIVE-2: 4/4 primary and all secondary
    endpoints met across active AND chronic TED
  - Elegrobart REVEAL-1: primary endpoint met today with p<0.0001

  and THRIVE-2 (56%) — not definitive but market noticed.

  concern. While a class effect, the rate is slightly elevated vs
  veligrotug, creating nervousness about REVEAL-2.
• SC delivery may have different tissue exposure kinetics vs IV
  infusion — PD/PK differences could modestly affect chronic TED
  response where tissue penetration matters more.

FACTOR 2: Trial Design / Risk Factors (Weight: 30%)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Score: 7.5/10

POSITIVES:
• REVEAL-2 design mirrors THRIVE-2 (the successful chronic TED trial
  for veligrotug):
  - Chronic TED population (onset >15 months)
  - Same primary endpoint (PRR at primary analysis timepoint)
  - Double-masked, placebo-controlled
• REVEAL-2 enrolled ~132 patients (per corporate presentation showing
  117 target enrollment for REVEAL-1, REVEAL-2 likely similar scale).
  [Source: Viridian Feb 2026 Corporate Deck, q4cdn.com]
• 24-week primary endpoint (vs 15-week for THRIVE-2) — longer
  assessment may allow MORE time for tissue remodeling response in
  chronic TED, potentially favoring efficacy.
• Q4W and Q8W dosing arms tested — provides two shots at success.
• Open-label crossover for non-responders at 24 weeks — ethical
  design, maintaining trial integrity.

NEGATIVES/RISKS:
• Chronic TED is biologically harder: more fibrotic tissue, less
  inflammatory drive. Effect sizes may attenuate vs active TED.
• REVEAL-1 Q4W PRR of 54% was below Street expectations (market
  sold off 32-41%). If REVEAL-2 shows further attenuation in chronic
  TED, even a "positive" result could disappoint commercially.
• Hearing impairment safety signal creates heightened scrutiny for
  REVEAL-2 safety data. Even if efficacy hits, a worsened safety
  profile in chronic TED could be problematic.
• The 24-week primary endpoint introduces more time for variability
  and potential dropout.
• Chronic TED patients have had disease longer — potential for more
  heterogeneous pathology and variable treatment response.

RATIONALE FOR 70% ESTIMATE:
─────────────────────────────
This is a moderately high-conviction score driven by:

1. VALIDATED MECHANISM: Anti-IGF-1R is the most proven mechanism in
   TED. Three successful Phase 3 programs (THRIVE, THRIVE-2, REVEAL-1)
   all met primary endpoints. The biology is established.

2. DIRECT ANALOG SUCCESS: Veligrotug THRIVE-2 (chronic TED) met ALL
   endpoints with robust effect sizes. Elegrobart shares the same
   binding domain. THRIVE → THRIVE-2 translation was seamless;
   REVEAL-1 → REVEAL-2 should follow a similar pattern.

3. REVEAL-1 CONFIRMED SC FORMULATION WORKS: 54% PRR, 63% Q8W PRR,
   51% diplopia resolution. The drug is active. The question for
   REVEAL-2 is degree, not direction.

4. CHRONIC TED BIOLOGY SUPPORTS ANTI-IGF-1R: Unlike purely anti-
   inflammatory agents, anti-IGF-1R addresses orbital fibroblast
   hyaluronan production and adipogenesis — pathways active in both
   active and chronic TED.

WHY NOT HIGHER (>75%):
• REVEAL-1 PRR of 54% Q4W was below veligrotug benchmarks, suggesting
  SC delivery may yield modestly lower efficacy. In chronic TED, this
  attenuation could push the effect size closer to marginal significance.
• Hearing impairment safety signal adds regulatory/commercial risk and
  creates negative sentiment around the entire program.
• Chronic TED has higher heterogeneity and more fibrotic pathology —
  inherently harder to treat than active TED.
• The 32-41% stock selloff today signals the market sees meaningful
  risk that REVEAL-2 could disappoint, even if it technically "meets."
• n=~132 is adequate but not massively overpowered — modest effect
  size attenuation could push below significance threshold.
