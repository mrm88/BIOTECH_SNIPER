>>> CRITICAL CLARIFICATION: PRIMARY ENDPOINT DISTINCTION <<<

The pivotal Phase 3 (Part 2) primary endpoint is NOT NSAA change from baseline.
The primary endpoint is: PROPORTION OF PARTICIPANTS WITH MICRODYSTROPHIN
EXPRESSION ≥10% AT WEEK 12 (biomarker/surrogate endpoint for accelerated approval).

Secondary endpoints include: NSAA, timed function tests (TTStand, 10MWR, TTClimb),
PDMS-3 (for <4yo), SV95C.

This is a CRITICAL distinction. REGENXBIO aligned with FDA on using microdystrophin
expression as a surrogate endpoint to support accelerated approval. This dramatically
changes the probability calculation vs. the EMBARK analogy (which used NSAA as primary).

Source: REGENXBIO pivotal enrollment completion PR (Oct 30, 2025); ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05693142;
Fierce Biotech (Mar 12, 2026); REGENXBIO Q4 2025 earnings (Mar 5, 2026).

(c) NSAA as Primary Endpoint — Historical Hit Rate
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sarepta EMBARK: MISSED (p=0.2441) — NSAA is notoriously variable
- NSAA has high inter-patient variability, especially in ambulatory DMD boys
- The 52-week timeframe compounds variability; maturational effects confound
- Industry consensus: NSAA is suboptimal as a primary for gene therapy in DMD
- RGNX is NOT using NSAA as primary — they learned from Sarepta's experience

NSAA as supportive/secondary: RGNX Phase I/II data is strong (see Section 2)

(d) Microdystrophin Expression Phase 3 Outcomes (Direct Analogy)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sarepta EMBARK: Mean microdystrophin expression 34.29% at Week 12 (treated)
  vs 0.00% (placebo). This was the basis for accelerated + traditional approval.
  Source: Nature Medicine (Oct 2024)
- RGNX Phase I/II: Expression 20% to 122% across pivotal dose patients (n=10+)
  ALL patients had expression >10% at Week 12
  Source: REGENXBIO PR (Mar 11, 2026); REGENXBIO PR (Oct 30, 2025)
- Newest patient (age 3.6): 51.2% at Week 12
  Source: REGENXBIO MDA presentation (Mar 2026)

Expression threshold of ≥10% is a LOW BAR given Phase I/II range of 20-122%.
This is by design — FDA aligned on this as the pivotal endpoint.

BASE RATE SYNTHESIS: ~75-85% given biomarker primary endpoint + orphan gene therapy
Phase 3 success rates + favorable precedent with ELEVIDYS expression data.

SECTION 2: FACTOR ANALYSIS (Weighted Scoring)
================================================================================

FACTOR 1: Quality of Phase 1/2 NSAA Data (30% weight)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Score: 8.5/10

Evidence (data cut Jan 5, 2026, n=7 pivotal dose at 12 months):
- NSAA improvement: +4.9 points vs cTAP model (all pivotal dose patients)
- Older patients (8+ years, n=5): +5.2 points vs cTAP
- 18-month data (n=4): +7.4 points vs cTAP (reported Jan 2026)
- Dose level 1 at 24 months (n=3): +5.6 points vs cTAP
- All timed function tests exceeded external controls
- 95% CI demonstrates favorability to RGX-202 vs propensity-weighted controls
- Exceeded MCID benchmarks referenced by FDA in ELEVIDYS approval

Comparison to Sarepta Phase 1/2:
- RGNX ~+4.9 NSAA vs cTAP at 12 months is STRONG
- Sarepta Phase 1/2 showed similar trajectory but smaller older patient cohort
- RGNX data in 8+ year olds (decline phase) is particularly compelling

Limitations:
- Small N (7 at pivotal dose), no randomized control
- External controls (propensity score weighting) have inherent limitations
- Selection bias in open-label early phase cannot be fully excluded

FACTOR 2: Endpoint Sensitivity / Statistical Power at N~30 (20% weight)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Score: 9.0/10

CRITICAL: The pivotal N is ~30 (not 60-70 as stated in the task briefing).
The ~30 additional patients are in the CONFIRMATORY trial (Part 3), not pivotal.

Primary endpoint: Proportion with microdystrophin ≥10% at Week 12
- This is a BINARY endpoint (yes/no per patient) with N~30
- In Phase I/II, 100% of patients (all tested) exceeded 10%
- Expression ranged 20-122% — lowest was still 2x the threshold
- Even with some manufacturing/patient variability, clearing 10% is highly likely
- Statistical test: likely exact binomial or similar proportion test
- If 28/30 or 29/30 hit ≥10%, this overwhelmingly meets endpoint

Power analysis:
- With expression floor of ~20% in Phase I/II, probability of any single
  patient failing to reach 10% is very low
- At N=30, even 2-3 failures still likely meets a pre-specified threshold
- This endpoint is effectively a binary pass/fail on biological proof of concept
- MUCH more statistically favorable than NSAA change-from-baseline

FACTOR 3: Competitive Differentiation vs ELEVIDYS (15% weight)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Score: 7.0/10

Advantages of RGX-202:
- C-Terminal (CT) domain inclusion: Only microdystrophin with CT domain,
  shown preclinically to improve DAPC restoration and muscle resilience
  Source: REGENXBIO data presentations
- Codon optimization: May improve expression efficiency
- Superior safety profile: NO SAEs, NO AESIs, NO liver injury signals in 13 patients
  vs. ELEVIDYS Phase 3: 22.2% SAE rate, 11.1% treatment-related SAEs
  Source: REGENXBIO PR (Mar 11, 2026); Nature Medicine EMBARK data
- Higher product purity: >80% full capsids (industry-leading)
- Higher vector genome copies: 4.9-55.4 vg/diploid genome (highest reported)
- Expression levels competitive or superior: 20-122% vs Sarepta's ~34% mean

Disadvantages:
- ELEVIDYS already approved and on market (~$3.2M per dose)
- ELEVIDYS has 3-year follow-up data showing durable benefit
  (NSAA +4.39 vs EC at Year 3, p=0.0002)
  Source: Sarepta PR (Jan 26, 2026)
- RGX-202 is second-to-market; label differentiation will matter
- FDA recently rejected RGNX's RGX-121 (Hunter syndrome) BLA — regulatory credibility risk
  Source: REGENXBIO CRL announcement (Feb 2026)

FACTOR 4: Manufacturing/Expression Consistency (15% weight)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Score: 8.5/10

- NAVXpress suspension-based manufacturing: proprietary, high-yield process
- >80% full capsid ratio (industry-leading; reduces empty capsid immunogenicity)
- Commercial manufacturing initiated at in-house Manufacturing Innovation Center
  Source: REGENXBIO PR (Oct 30, 2025)
- Expression consistency: ALL Phase I/II patients showed robust expression
  - Range: 20-122% (pivotal dose, N=10+)
  - Age range tested: 1 to 12 years
  - Youngest patient (3.6 yo): 51.2% — still 5x the threshold
  - 100% sarcolemmal localization confirmed
  Source: REGENXBIO MDA presentation (Mar 2026)
- Full drug supply ready for confirmatory trial
- AAV8 is well-characterized vector (used in Zolgensma franchise)

Risk factor:
- Pivotal cohort is larger (N~30) vs Phase I/II expression data (N~13)
- Manufacturing scale-up from clinical to commercial batches could introduce variability
- But expression threshold is LOW (10%) relative to demonstrated range (20-122%)

FACTOR 5: Patient Population Selection (20% weight)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Score: 7.5/10

Strengths:
- Ambulatory boys aged 1+ (broad age range increases generalizability)
- Pivotal dose Phase I/II included patients 5.8-12.1 years at dosing
- 5/7 pivotal dose patients were 8+ (decline phase) — showing benefit in
  hardest-to-treat ambulatory subgroup
- Stable corticosteroid requirement (12 weeks) standardizes baseline
- Genetic confirmation required (exons 18+)
- AAV8 seronegative screening

Risks:
- Open-label design: ALL patients know they received treatment (placebo effect on
  functional measures, though irrelevant for biomarker primary endpoint)
- Broad age range (1 to 12+) introduces heterogeneity in functional assessments
- ~13% AAV seropositive exclusion rate (based on Sarepta's AAVrh74 data; AAV8
  may differ but cross-reactivity exists)
- Disease stage variability within ambulatory DMD population
